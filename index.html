How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve a stranger's driveway.

Campus marketplaces already exist — they're just buried in group chats and classifieds built for a whole city. Axis is the version scoped to people you already share a library with.

STEP 01

Verify once

Sign up with your @uwo.ca address. A six-digit code confirms you're a student — the only thing standing between the marketplace and the rest of the internet.

STEP 02

Post in a minute

Photos, a price, the condition, and where you'll hand it over. Mark it negotiable or open to trades.

STEP 03

Meet on campus

Message in the app, pick a busy spot between classes, settle it in person. Axis never touches the money.

Safety

A small marketplace can afford to be strict.

Every protection here is enforced on our servers, not just in the app.

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Axis is a marketplace built for verified university students. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because you deserve to actually read it.

Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name and your Western-issued @uwo.ca (or @alumni.uwo.ca) email address, which we use to confirm you are part of the Western community.

As you use Axis we also store:

  • The listings you post — titles, prices, photos, and descriptions.
  • The messages you send to other students.
  • Basic activity such as the items you save or view.
  • Your profile details, including an optional photo and bio.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or any advertising identifier. Axis contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

We use your information to verify your student status, show your listings to other buyers, deliver messages, keep the marketplace safe, and improve the app. We may send you service notifications about your account, your listings, or your conversations.

How we share information

Your name and active listings are visible to other verified students so they can buy from you. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Two service providers process data on our behalf, and both are contractually required to protect it:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in.
  • Sentry receives crash reports when the app fails. Crash reports contain the technical details of the failure and your device model — never your name, email, messages, or listings.

We share data otherwise only when required by law or to protect the safety of our community.

How we protect it

Photos you attach to a listing are served from a public web address, so anyone who has that address can open the image — that is what lets listings load quickly for every buyer. Your profile photo is not: it is stored privately and shown only to signed-in Axis members, through a link that expires.

Your sign-in session is encrypted on your device and the key is held in the operating system's secure keychain. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced row by row in the database, so one account cannot read another's private data even if the app is modified.

Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile and listings, though we may retain limited records where needed to resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your listings at any time, update your profile details, and block anyone you no longer want to hear from. You can delete your account from the Settings screen inside the app, which permanently removes your profile, listings, photos, and messages.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Email us and we will help.

Children

Axis is intended for university students and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be surfaced in the app.

Questions about your privacy? Reach our team at axis.app@outlook.com and we will get back to you.

Support

We reply to every message within 24 hours

Something broken, someone behaving badly, or a question about your account? Email axis.app@outlook.com and a real person will get back to you. The answers to the most common questions are below.

Report a listing, a profile, or a conversation

Every piece of content in Axis can be reported from the screen you found it on:

  • A listing — open it and tap the flag icon at the top right.
  • A person — open their profile and tap the ··· button at the top right.
  • A conversation — open the chat and tap the flag icon in the header.

Pick a reason — spam, a prohibited item, harassment, or something else — and send it. Reports are confidential: the person you report is never told who filed it. Our team reviews every report and responds within 24 hours. Content that breaks our community guidelines is removed, and the accounts behind it are suspended or permanently banned.

Block someone

The same sheet that files a report also offers to block. Blocking is immediate and mutual: you disappear from each other's feed, search results, and inbox, and neither of you can start a new conversation with the other. You do not have to file a report to block someone.

To see who you have blocked, or to unblock them, open Settings → Blocked users.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This is immediate and cannot be undone. It permanently removes your profile, your listings, every photo you uploaded, your messages, your saved items, and your blocks.

One thing to know before you do it: to keep deletion from being used to escape a block or a report history, the same email address cannot create a new Axis account for 14 days afterwards. If you only want a break, signing out is the better option.

Trouble signing up or signing in

  • Axis only accepts @uwo.ca and @alumni.uwo.ca addresses. Any other address is turned away at signup — this is the check that keeps the marketplace to students.
  • Signing up sends a six-digit code to that address. If it has not arrived after a minute, check your junk folder before requesting another.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen, or Settings → Change password if you are already signed in. Both send a code to your student address.

Staying safe on campus

Axis never holds money and takes no fee — every deal is settled in person, which is why we ask you to meet in a busy public spot on campus during daylight, inspect an item before you pay, and share no more personal information than the handoff needs. If a conversation makes you uncomfortable, block first and report second; you do not owe anyone an explanation.

Still stuck? Email axis.app@outlook.com. Include your student email address and, if it helps, a screenshot — it gets you a faster answer.

Community guidelines

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Axis works because students look out for each other. These guidelines keep the marketplace welcoming, honest, and safe. Break them and you may lose access to Axis.

Be respectful

Treat every student the way you would want to be treated. Keep messages polite, communicate clearly, and honor the deals you agree to. A little courtesy goes a long way on a campus you share.

No prohibited or illegal items

Do not list anything illegal or unsafe, including alcohol, drugs, weapons, stolen goods, counterfeit items, or anything that violates your school's policies. If it wouldn't be allowed on campus, it doesn't belong on Axis.

No harassment or scams

Harassment, hate speech, threats, and discrimination are never tolerated. Do not attempt to scam other students, post fake listings, ask for payment outside an agreed exchange, or pressure anyone into a deal.

Nothing sexually explicit

Listings, photos, profiles, reviews, and messages must stay free of sexually explicit or otherwise objectionable content. This is enforced on our servers, not just in the app — some language is rejected the moment you try to post it.

Meet safely on campus

Arrange to meet in busy, public spots on campus during daylight, such as the student centre or library. Inspect items before you pay, and never share more personal information than a transaction requires.

Reporting

If you see a listing or message that breaks these guidelines, report it so our team can review it. You can also block users you no longer want to hear from. Reports are confidential and help keep Axis safe for everyone. How to report or block →

Our team reviews every report and responds within 24 hours. Content that violates these guidelines is removed, and the accounts responsible for it are suspended or permanently banned from Axis.

For anything urgent, or if you'd rather reach us directly, email axis.app@outlook.com.
