How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve a stranger's driveway.

Campus marketplaces already exist — they're just buried in group chats and classifieds built for a whole city. Axis is the version scoped to people you already share a library with.

STEP 01

Verify once

Sign up with your @uwo.ca address. A six-digit code confirms you're a student — the only thing standing between the marketplace and the rest of the internet.

STEP 02

Post in a minute

Photos, a price, the condition, and where you'll hand it over. Mark it negotiable or open to trades.

STEP 03

Meet on campus

Message in the app, pick a busy spot between classes, settle it in person. Axis never touches the money.

Safety

A small marketplace can afford to be strict.

Every protection here is enforced on our servers, not just in the app.

Students only

The email domain is checked at signup and again in the database, so an account can't exist without a real student address behind it.

Report and block

Every listing, profile, and conversation carries both. Block someone and you disappear from each other's feed and inbox immediately.

No money to intercept

Axis never holds funds and takes no fee. Deals settle in person, which removes the entire category of payment scams.

Your campus already has everything you need.

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Axis is a marketplace built for verified university students. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because you deserve to actually read it.

Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name and your Western-issued @uwo.ca (or @alumni.uwo.ca) email address, which we use to confirm you are part of the Western community.

As you use Axis we also store:

  • The listings you post — titles, prices, photos, and descriptions.
  • The messages you send to other students.
  • Basic activity such as the items you save or view.
  • Your profile details, including an optional photo and bio.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or any advertising identifier. Axis contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

We use your information to verify your student status, show your listings to other buyers, deliver messages, keep the marketplace safe, and improve the app. We may send you service notifications about your account, your listings, or your conversations.

How we share information

Your name and active listings are visible to other verified students so they can buy from you. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Two service providers process data on our behalf, and both are contractually required to protect it:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in.
  • Sentry receives crash reports when the app fails. Crash reports contain the technical details of the failure and your device model — never your name, email, messages, or listings.

We share data otherwise only when required by law or to protect the safety of our community.

How we protect it

Your sign-in session is encrypted on your device and the key is held in the operating system's secure keychain. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced row by row in the database, so one account cannot read another's private data even if the app is modified.

Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile and listings, though we may retain limited records where needed to resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your listings at any time, update your profile details, and block anyone you no longer want to hear from. You can delete your account from the Settings screen inside the app, which permanently removes your profile, listings, photos, and messages.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Email us and we will help.

Children

Axis is intended for university students and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be surfaced in the app.

Questions about your privacy? Reach our team at axis.app@outlook.com and we will get back to you.
